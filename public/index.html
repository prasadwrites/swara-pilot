    How to play

    An instrument plays one note. Tap its name on the keyboard. Right answers lift your parachute; a wrong answer drops you 90 m, and you sink slowly while you think. Six right answers take you to the next level, which adds another octave of notes.

    • Replay plays the note again and costs 15 m.
    • Hear a C plays a reference C on the same instrument, so you can count up or down from it.
    • Stats (top right) shows score, streak, altitude, level and best.
    • Keyboard (top right) moves the keys: below the game, floating (drag its bar, then lock it), or as a side panel with low notes at the bottom and high notes at the top.

    Computer keys: C D E F G A B answer · Shift for sharps (Shift+F = F#) · Space replays
