    Ear Trainer

    A random instrument plays one note. Pick which note it is. Right answers lift your parachute; wrong ones drop you toward the sea, and you sink slowly while you think. Touch the water and the round is over.

    InstrumentWhich note is this?

    Keyboard: press C D E F G A B to answer, hold Shift for sharps (Shift+F = F#). Space replays.

    Each round is a single note from the 12 (C to B), in a random octave and on a random instrument. "Hear a C" plays a reference C on the same instrument so you can compare the two and count up or down.
